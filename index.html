Yuri Bizzoni

Computational linguist, trained in classical literature and digital humanities.

I study literature with computational methods. My main areas of interest are stylistics, literary quality and how readers judge it, the diachronic evolution of literary language, and emotion and sentiment analysis of narrative texts, from sentiment arcs of whole novels to implicit sentiment in a single story.

Currently at Aarhus University, working on the Fabula-NET project: computational methods for estimating reader appreciation of literary fiction.
